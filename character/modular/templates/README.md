# Loafling starter templates

These files are recipes, not baked character images. Each recipe points to the
same reusable body, marking, expression, cloud and wearable modules used by the
composer.

## Basic templates

- `basic_classic.json`: canonical neutral Loafling.
- `basic_chubby.json`: rounder, heavier and extra squishy.
- `basic_long.json`: wider, flatter and more relaxed.
- `basic_bun.json`: a slightly taller, risen-dough silhouette.
- `basic_pudgy.json`: lower and softer, with a gently melted outline.

## Reference-inspired mutation templates

- `mutation_patchy.json`: adds `marking_patchy.svg` above the body while keeping
  the normal face, cloud and wearable slots available.
- `mutation_twin_cloud.json`: uses `cloud_twin.svg`, a blue and peach pair of
  floating clouds, while keeping the underlying Loafling unchanged.
- `mutation_sesame.json`: round mocha body, sesame-like flecks and warm cloud.
- `mutation_dapple.json`: softly pointed strawberry body, pastel patches and pink cloud.
- `mutation_sprout_cloud.json`: low melted matcha body and a tiny sprout on the cloud.

## Role outfit recipes

- `role_gardener.json`: leaf clip, sprout cloud and sage pinafore.
- `role_baker.json`: cream baker hat and a short apron.
- `role_stargazer.json`: lilac star cap, half-moon glasses and cocoa cardigan.
- `role_maker.json`: rose ribbon, amber glasses and blueberry overalls.
- `role_reader.json`: matcha body, bookmark clip, round glasses and book pocket.
- `role_tea_host.json`: mocha body, teacup, freckles and sage apron.
- `role_traveler.json`: strawberry body, bucket hat, honey glasses and blue satchel.
- `role_painter.json`: bun body, lilac painter hat, paint specks and peach smock.

These are ready-made visual combinations. They do not add job mechanics or
change the way a Loafling is generated.

`rarityHint` is art metadata only. It does not change the current settlement
probabilities or runtime rarity rules.
